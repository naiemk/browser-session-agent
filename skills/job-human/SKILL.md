# Human-attention session

1. Group inbox items by kind and site. Durable decisions can be answered without the browser.
2. For perishable challenges, re-drive one item to a fresh blocking point. If the blocker is gone, resolve and continue.
3. Never keep OTPs or CAPTCHA contents. Never solve them automatically. Never prepare two live challenges at once.
4. After the operator acts, verify the expected postcondition, then park or resume.

Headless sessions cannot take over a live challenge; tell the operator to run headed.
